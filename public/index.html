          How BIOBUZZ scores

          Everything the scorer uses, from the 2026–27 Competition Manual. Tap a part of the field.

          Schematic, not to scale. Audience side at the bottom.

          Match clock

          §10.1
          0:30 AUTO
          0:08
          2:00 TELEOP
          last 1:00 — NECTAR may enter FLOWERS
          • Tips completed before TELEOP starts count as AUTO. A TIP is still assessed while launched elements come to rest after the buzzer.
          • Elements launched during the transition or after the buzzer don't score.
          • A robot may control at most 4 elements. Controlling 5+ is a violation (G415).
          • The HIVE is a seesaw with two CELLS. Each TIP spills the full CELL and turns the empty one upward.
          • The top-most NECTAR owns a FLOWER; the owner scores 2 for every element inside, whoever put it there.
          • MINOR FOUL: 5 to the opponent. MAJOR FOUL: 20 to the opponent.
